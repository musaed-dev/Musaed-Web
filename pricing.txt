# Pricing — Musaed (مساعد)

The free plan covers all of Musaed's protection and moderation, with no card required.
Pro is an optional tier that adds Musaed's own name and picture inside a server, scheduled
messages, a custom embed style and a custom welcome card background, and raises some
per-server limits. The landing page's #plans panel shows Pro as "coming soon" with no numeric
price; the limits below come straight from the bot's plans table and are more detailed
than what the panel itself shows.

Last updated: 2026-11-22

## Free

- Price: 0
- Included, at full access: moderation, automatic protection and raid alerts, anti-nuke with the one-press restore, account-age gate, captcha verification, welcome and goodbye messages with a picture card, invite tracking (who invited whom), auto-roles, a self-roles panel, support tickets, auto-responses, text shortcuts, trap channel, server log, and /checkup with broken-feature alerts
- Text shortcuts: up to 7
- Ticket types: up to 6
- Ticket panels: 1
- Quick-open buttons per panel: not available
- Auto-responses: up to 20
- Auto-response reply length: up to 15 words
- Auto-responses carrying a file (image, PDF or text, up to 10 MB): 1, counted within the 20
- Welcome card background: the bot's own
- Embed style: the bot's default colour
- Musaed's name and picture in the server: the bot's own
- Scheduled messages: not available

## Pro

- Price: not yet announced. Assigned by the Musaed team directly; there is no self-serve
  checkout or purchase flow yet.
- Text shortcuts: up to 14
- Ticket panels: up to 3
- Quick-open buttons per panel: up to 3
- Auto-responses: up to 40
- Auto-response reply length: up to 50 words
- Auto-responses carrying a file: up to 20, counted within the 40
- Welcome card background: the server's own image
- Embed style: custom (brand colour + footer)
- Musaed's name, avatar, banner and bio inside the server: custom
- Scheduled messages (repeating posts): up to 5
- Ticket types: unchanged at 6

## Notes

- Early Access is not a Pro perk. It grants no higher limits, and its own features may change or become paid.
- Full limits and terms for each plan are governed by https://musaed.dev/terms.html and https://musaed.dev/privacy.html.
