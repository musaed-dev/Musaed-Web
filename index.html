01 — Protection

Five layers. One quiet server.

Each layer catches what slipped past the one before it. Most raiders never make it past the first.

01 Age gate

Brand-new accounts don't get in.

Set a minimum account age. Anything younger is kicked the moment it joins, with a DM explaining why, before it ever sees a captcha.

02 Captcha

A quick check before they get in.

New members get an Unverified role and a challenge in the verification channel, or by DM if you choose: one button, or a math question at easy, medium or hard. Too many wrong answers and they're out. Lockdown hides every channel except verification.

05 Anti-nuke

Stopped before the server is gone.

If someone starts deleting channels or roles, or mass-banning or mass-kicking members, Musaed removes their roles right away and alerts your staff. It's on from the moment you add the bot, and you can turn it off.

    05 — Your data

    Your data leaves when you do.

    Musaed keeps what it needs to protect your server, and clears the rest on a schedule.

    1. Removed the bot? Your setup waits.

      If Musaed leaves your server, its data is set aside, not kept forever. Add it back soon and your setup returns. Wait too long and it's deleted for good.

    2. Verification records clear in hours.

      Captcha and age-gate records are working data, cleared within hours. The mod log keeps the permanent record of any kick.

    3. Old history is pruned.

      Old moderation history is cleaned up on a schedule, so nothing about your members sits around forever.

    07 — Questions

    Before you add it.

    Does Musaed speak Arabic?

    Yes. Musaed was built Arabic-first. Its replies, panels and command descriptions are all in Arabic.

    Where do I change settings?

    In the web dashboard. Sign in with Discord, pick your server, and every feature has its own page. Ticket and verification panels in Discord update on their own when you save.

    Will the age gate kick real people?

    Only accounts younger than the minimum you choose, and only when they join. They get a DM explaining why, so a real person knows to come back once their account is old enough.

    What does the honeypot catch that the captcha doesn't?

    Accounts that already passed verification: captcha-solving bots, hijacked accounts, or anyone ignoring a channel labelled "don't post here". Unverified accounts can't even see the trap.

    When is Pro coming?

    Soon. Everything in the free plan works today. Pro will add higher limits, your server's own colour and footer on Musaed's messages, and more than one ticket panel. We'll announce the launch in the support server.

    Can I use Musaed with other bots?

    Yes. Every feature can be turned on or off by itself, so Musaed can guard the door while your other bots keep their jobs.

    What is Early Access?

    A way for a server to try new Musaed features before they launch for everyone. It never gives more than Pro and never raises a limit. A feature in it can change or be removed, and it can become paid later. We'll announce how to join in the support server.
