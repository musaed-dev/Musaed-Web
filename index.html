02 · Captcha

A quick check before they get in.

New members get an Unverified role and a challenge in the verification channel, or by DM if you choose: one button, or a math question at easy, medium or hard. Too many wrong answers and they're out. Lockdown hides every channel except verification.

03 · Honeypot

A trap only bots fall into.

Pick one channel no real member would post in. Any account that writes there is muted, kicked or banned automatically, and you choose whether its message is deleted or stays. It catches captcha-solving bots and hijacked accounts.

04 · AutoMod

Spam, banned words and raids, handled.

Banned words are caught even stretched out, spaced apart, swapped for symbols or typed in Arabizi, and in messages edited after sending. A ready-made list from the Musaed team covers several dialects, and you can turn it off. Spam detection and raid detection run alongside.

05 · Anti-nuke

Stopped before the server is gone.

If someone starts deleting channels or roles, mass-banning or mass-kicking, creating webhooks or adding bots, Musaed removes their roles right away, kicks the bots they added and alerts your staff. The alert has a button: press it within 10 minutes and Musaed puts back the channels and roles they deleted and lifts their bans. Give @everyone a dangerous permission and they're banned on the spot, with the permission taken back. It's on from the moment you add the bot, and you can turn it off.

Before you add it.

Does Musaed speak Arabic?

Yes. Musaed was built Arabic-first. Its replies, panels and command descriptions are all in Arabic.

Where do I change settings?

In the web dashboard. Sign in with Discord, pick your server, and every feature has its own page. Ticket and verification panels in Discord update on their own when you save.

Will the age gate kick real people?

Only accounts younger than the minimum you choose, and only when they join. They get a DM explaining why, so a real person knows to come back once their account is old enough.

What does the honeypot catch that the captcha doesn't?

Accounts that already passed verification: captcha-solving bots, hijacked accounts, or anyone ignoring a channel labelled "don't post here". Unverified accounts can't even see the trap.

When is Pro coming?

Soon. Everything in the free plan works today. Pro will add Musaed's own name and picture inside your server, messages that repeat on a schedule, your server's own colour and footer on Musaed's messages, more than one ticket panel, and higher limits. We'll announce the launch in the support server.

Can I use Musaed with other bots?

Yes. Every feature can be turned on or off by itself, so Musaed can guard the door while your other bots keep their jobs.

What is Early Access?

A way for a server to try new Musaed features before they launch for everyone. It never gives more than Pro and never raises a limit. A feature in it can change or be removed, and it can become paid later. We'll announce how to join in the support server.

What do you keep about my server?

Only what Musaed needs to protect your server, and the rest is cleared on a schedule. If you remove the bot, its data is set aside: add it back soon and your setup returns, wait too long and it's deleted for good. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Why does Musaed ask for Administrator?

To stop someone who is wrecking your server, Musaed's role has to sit above theirs, and it needs to take roles away and bring deleted channels and roles back. Every action it takes is written to the mod log. And turn on two-factor authentication for all your staff: most wrecked servers start with a hacked mod account.
